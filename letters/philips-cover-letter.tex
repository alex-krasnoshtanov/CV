Dear Hiring Manager,

I am applying for the Intern AI Application Developer position in the
Healthcare Consultancy Service team. The role asks for AI tools that
non-technical colleagues can pick up and rely on, delivered as something
pilotable rather than a proof of concept. Most of what I have built during my
studies fits that description, and it is the work I want to do more of.

% Why Philips: museum visit and Brainport are the specifics; the transformation
% line links Philips' history to the healthcare team this role sits in.
Philips is a company whose values I share, and visiting the Philips Museum in
Eindhoven made that even more concrete: more than a century of reinventing what it makes,
from light bulbs to health technology. At BrabantHack 2026 I also saw the
Brainport ecosystem that grew around Philips, and I want to work for a company
that builds for the long term.

In a two-person sprint I built a self-hosted RAG assistant that answers event
questions from a client's confidential documents through a Gradio web
interface; the client deployed it to production. Reliability came
from constraining the model rather than trusting it: answers are drawn only
from retrieved context, each is returned with its source passages and their
relevance, and the assistant says so explicitly when the documents do not
cover a question.

On a five-person platform project I owned deployment and CI/CD for a FastAPI
and Next.js application:
one codebase shipping to Docker Compose, on-premise Portainer and Azure
Container Apps, with blue-green cutover, health-gated rollback, an 85\%
coverage gate and 430 tests. Separately, I wrote a real-time sign-language
trainer end to end on my own, from the PyTorch models to a FastAPI WebSocket
backend and a bilingual Next.js frontend.

The consultancy side of the role matches my current project. As data engineer
on the Measurement Trust Project, I work on inconsistent item measurement at
bol's fulfilment centres BFC1/2, which bol owns and CEVA Logistics operates.
Much of the work so far has been with people rather than code: obtaining
partly anonymised data from both CEVA and bol, working with Logistore and other
third parties, writing business requirements documents, and keeping proposals
compliant with GDPR and ethics requirements while making them understandable
to non-technical stakeholders.
