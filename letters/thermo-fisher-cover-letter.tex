Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing about the Gen AI Assistant internship with the Engineering team in
Eindhoven --- profiling Docgraph for retrieval time, evaluating existing
guardrail implementations, reducing hallucinations, and moving a proof of
concept into the MSD R\&D environment.

In 2025 I built a self-hosted retrieval assistant for MARBET, a corporate events
agency, in a two-person sprint; they run it in production. It answers from the
client's own confidential documents, which is why it runs on-premise: Llama 3.3
70B (q5\_K\_M) through Ollama, orchestrated with LangChain over
mxbai-embed-large embeddings in FAISS. Most of the effort went into answer
quality rather than the model. I chunked semantically on paragraph and then
sentence boundaries instead of fixed cuts, used two-stage fetch-then-filter
retrieval, returned the source passages with every answer, and made the
assistant say plainly when the documents did not cover a question rather than
fill the gap. A guided assistant for Smart EPU has the same failure mode with
higher stakes: a confident wrong answer about which squares or foil holes to
collect from costs a user a microscope session, while an honest ``not covered''
costs them a click.

The on-prem constraint is part of why the work appeals to me. Your software
lives on customer infrastructure --- Embedded CryoSPARC Live on the microscope
hardware, Athena inside an existing on-premises environment --- and a pharma
customer's structures are not going to a third-party API. I have shipped into
that shape: on an MLOps platform for NPEC I ran one codebase to both on-premise
Portainer and Azure Container Apps, with blue-green cutover and health-gated
rollback. Getting a proof of concept to survive the move into a controlled R\&D
environment is a specific skill, and it is the one I expect would be most
immediately useful.

Where I would want to start is measurement: a labelled question set over the
real documentation, retrieval recall and latency measured separately from
generation, so that every later change to chunking, reranking or guardrails can
be argued with a number instead of an impression. It is also the prerequisite
for the hallucination work.

I am available from February 2027 and would be glad to talk through any of this.
